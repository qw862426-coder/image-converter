Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information PicShifter (picshifter.pages.dev) handles and how.

Your files

Files you choose in our tools are processed only inside your browser. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. When you close the page, they are gone from the page’s memory.

Information stored on your device

We save the language you choose in your browser’s local storage so the site opens in that language next time. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before personalized ads or related cookies are used.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
