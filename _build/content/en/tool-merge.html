When to merge PDFs

  • Sending one file instead of five: a CV, cover letter and certificates for a job application.
  • Putting scanned receipts or invoices together for an expense report.
  • Combining chapters or handouts into one document to print.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose your PDFs. You can add JPG and PNG photos too; each becomes a page.
  2. Use the arrows to put the files in order.
  3. Press Merge into one PDF and save the file.

Frequently asked questions

Does merging lower the quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so text, images and links inside the pages keep their quality.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Open the file with its password and save a copy without one first.

Can I change the page order inside a file?

Use Split PDF to pull out the pages you need, then merge the pieces in the order you want.
