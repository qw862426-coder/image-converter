Why send Word files as PDF

A Word file can look different on someone else’s computer if they have other fonts or another version of Word, and it can be edited by mistake. A PDF looks the same everywhere, which is why job applications, schools and offices usually ask for one.

How to convert Word to PDF

  1. Choose your .docx files above. You can add several at once.
  2. Press Convert to PDF and wait for the progress bar.
  3. Press Save to download each PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Will the layout stay the same?

Text, tables, pictures and page breaks are kept. Complex layouts, text boxes and fonts that aren’t on your device may look slightly different, so check the PDF before you send it.

Can I select the text in the PDF?

No. Pages are saved as images, so the text can’t be selected or searched.

What about old .doc files?

Open them in Word or another editor and save as .docx first.

Keep Word or send a PDF?

Send the .docx when the other person needs to edit the text, for example a draft you are writing together. Send a PDF when the document is finished: a CV, a cover letter, homework or a signed form. The reader sees exactly the pages you checked, and nobody can change a word by accident.

Tips for a cleaner PDF

  • Use common fonts such as Arial, Calibri or Times New Roman. A font that is not on your device is replaced by a similar one, which can move lines and pages.
  • Keep important text in normal paragraphs and tables. Floating text boxes and shapes are the parts most likely to shift.
  • Where a new page must start, insert a page break (Ctrl + Enter) instead of pressing Enter many times. Page breaks are kept in the PDF.

Turning an old .doc into .docx

  • Microsoft Word: File → Save As → choose “Word Document (*.docx)”.
  • Google Docs: open the file, then File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx).
  • LibreOffice Writer: File → Save As → choose the Word 2007–365 (.docx) type.

More questions

Do links in my document still work?

No. Each page is saved as a picture, so links can’t be clicked. If the reader needs a link, write the full address so it can be typed.

Why is the PDF bigger than my Word file?

A .docx stores text, while this PDF stores a picture of every page. Long documents therefore make larger PDFs.
