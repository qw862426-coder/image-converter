What you can remove

  • People walking through the back of a travel photo
  • Wires, signs, litter and parked scooters
  • Text, dates, watermarks you own and small logos
  • Spots, crumbs and blemishes on product photos

How to get a clean result

  1. Paint a little wider than the object. Leftover edges and shadows are the most common reason a faint outline stays behind.
  2. Remove big things in steps. Paint half of a large person, press Erase, then do the rest. Each step gives the AI more background to copy from.
  3. Zoom by cropping. For a tiny object in a huge photo, results are sharper if you crop first in the photo editor.
  4. Compare. Hold “See the original” to check the change, and use Undo if you don’t like it.

How it works

The page cuts out a square around what you painted, shrinks it to 512 × 512 pixels and gives it to the AI: in high quality mode Big LaMa from Samsung AI researchers (strong at filling large areas), in fast mode MI-GAN from Picsart AI Research, built to run on phones. The model looks at the unpainted pixels around the hole and draws what most likely belongs there. Only the painted area is pasted back into your full-size photo, with softened edges, so the rest of the picture stays untouched.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a large area look blurry?

The model works at 512 pixels. A big area in a high-resolution photo is enlarged afterwards, so it can look softer than the rest. Erasing in smaller pieces helps.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser once and runs there.

Can I remove someone else’s watermark?

Only remove watermarks from photos you have the right to use. Removing a watermark doesn’t give you permission to use someone else’s picture.
