Why share slides as PDF

Presentation files are large and depend on the fonts and version of PowerPoint on each computer. A PDF opens on any phone or laptop and is the usual way to hand out slides after a class or meeting.

How to convert PowerPoint to PDF

  1. Choose your .pptx files above.
  2. Press Convert to PDF.
  3. Save the PDF. Each slide becomes one landscape page.

What to expect

  • Text, shapes, pictures, tables and slide backgrounds are included.
  • Animations, transitions, videos and speaker notes are not.
  • Complex charts, SmartArt and special effects may look different from PowerPoint.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert old .ppt files?

Not directly. Open the file in PowerPoint, Keynote or another editor and save it as .pptx first.

Can I edit the PDF afterwards?

The pages are saved as images, so the text can’t be edited or selected.

Making good handouts

A PDF is the easiest way to share slides after a class, meeting or talk: it opens on any phone, it is much easier to print, and nobody needs PowerPoint. Because speaker notes are not included, put anything the audience must remember on the slides themselves.

What happens to animations

A PDF page can’t move. Objects that appear one by one in PowerPoint are all shown together on the slide’s page, in the positions they have on the slide. If items overlap during the animation, check that page before sharing.

Turning an old .ppt into .pptx

  • PowerPoint: File → Save As → choose “PowerPoint Presentation (*.pptx)”.
  • Google Slides: File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx).
  • Keynote: File → Export To → PowerPoint.

More questions

Are 16:9 and 4:3 slides both supported?

Yes. Each slide becomes one landscape page, whatever the slide size.

Why is the PDF large?

Every slide is saved as a picture. Decks with many photos make bigger PDFs. For a smaller file, use smaller photos in the deck or share only the slides people need.
