When to split a PDF

  • Sending only the pages someone needs from a long report or contract.
  • Removing blank pages, cover pages or pages with personal details before sharing.
  • Breaking a scanned stack into one file per document or per page.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF. Small previews of every page appear.
  2. Tap the pages you want, or type numbers like 1-3, 5, 8- (the last one means “8 to the end”).
  3. Choose Keep selected pages, Delete selected pages or Every page as its own PDF.
  4. Press Save PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does the text stay searchable?

Yes. Pages are copied exactly, not turned into pictures.

What do I get with “Every page as its own PDF”?

One ZIP file containing a PDF for each page, numbered in order.

Can I join pages from different files?

Yes, with Merge PDF.
