What it reads

  • Korean: all 2,350 common Hangul syllables (the KS X 1001 set) and single consonants and vowels such as ㅋㅋ.
  • English letters plus accented letters used in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Polish and Vietnamese (é, ü, ñ, ç, ß, ế…), numbers and common symbols: . , ! ? ( ) / % ₩ € and more.
  • Printed text in screenshots, scanned documents, receipts and signs photographed straight on.

How to get the best result

  1. Use a screenshot or a scan if you can. They are much sharper than photos.
  2. For a photo, shoot straight on in good light, then crop with the box so only the text is read.
  3. Very small text is enlarged automatically, but text under about 12 pixels tall still causes mistakes.

How it works

This text reader was built by PicShifter. It finds the dark marks on the image, groups them into lines, tries different ways to split each line into letters, and compares each letter shape with a model and a small neural network trained on many Korean and Latin fonts. The best-fitting combination becomes the text. Nothing is sent to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Can it read handwriting or Chinese characters?

Not yet. It is designed for printed Korean and English.

Is it as accurate as paid OCR apps?

On clear screenshots it gets most characters right, but large commercial OCR systems are still more accurate on difficult photos. Always check the text before you use it.
