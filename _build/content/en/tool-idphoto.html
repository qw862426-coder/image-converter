For a Korean passport, the head must measure 3.2–3.6 cm from the crown to the chin, the background must be plain white, and the photo must have been taken within the last six months. Edited photos and composited backgrounds are not accepted.

How to make your photo

  1. Pick the size for your document.
  2. Choose a photo taken from the front, at eye level, against a plain light background.
  3. Move and resize the frame until the top of the head and the chin sit inside the yellow bands.
  4. Save the single photo for online applications, or the 4×6 sheet to print at a photo shop or at home.

Tips for a photo that gets accepted

  • Stand about 1–1.5 m from the camera and use the back camera with someone else holding the phone. Selfies distort the face.
  • Light your face evenly from the front so there are no shadows on the face or behind you.
  • Keep a neutral expression, both eyes open, mouth closed, and hair away from the eyes.
  • Remove glasses if the rules ask you to; many countries now require it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the background to white here?

The background remover can make a white background, but many authorities, including Korea’s passport office, don’t accept photos with an edited background. Taking the photo against a white wall is the safer choice.

Why does my printed photo come out the wrong size?

Print the 4×6 sheet at 100% scale with “fit to page” turned off.
