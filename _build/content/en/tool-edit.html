What you can do

  • Crop and rotate: cut to 1:1 for profile pictures, 4:5 for Instagram posts, 9:16 for stories, or any shape you like. Rotate and mirror in one tap.
  • Adjust: brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, fade, vignette and film grain.
  • Filters: Classic, Pop, Film, Retro, Aura, Vintage, Cinema and more, each with a strength slider.
  • Mosaic and blur: paint over faces, car plates, addresses or phone numbers to hide them before you post.
  • Text and watermarks: add a caption, or repeat your name across the whole photo so it can’t easily be reused.
  • Resize: set exact pixels or shrink to 25, 50 or 75%.

How to hide faces or personal details

  1. Open the Mosaic & blur tab.
  2. Choose Mosaic or Blur and set the brush size.
  3. Paint over what you want to hide. The painted area shows in red.
  4. Press Apply, then Save.

The pixels under the mosaic are replaced in the saved file, so the hidden details can’t be recovered from it. Higher strength hides more.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo?

Yes. Undo steps back through your last few changes, and Start over returns to the original photo.

Will my photo lose quality?

Saving as PNG keeps every pixel. For JPG and WebP, a quality of 90% or higher looks the same as the original to most people.

Can I remove a person or object from a photo?

That needs a different kind of AI tool, which we are working on. For now you can crop the area out, or cover it with mosaic or blur.
