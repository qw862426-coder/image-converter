What is an HWP file?

HWP is the document format of Hangul (Hancom Office), the word processor widely used by Korean government offices, schools and companies. People outside Korea, and many Mac and phone users, can’t open HWP files without installing extra software. Converting to PDF lets anyone read and print them.

How to convert HWP to PDF

  1. Choose your .hwp files above.
  2. Press Convert to PDF.
  3. Save the PDF. Pages keep the paper size set in the document, usually A4.

Frequently asked questions

Are HWPX files supported?

Not yet. In Hangul, use “Save as” and choose the HWP format, then convert.

Will it look exactly like Hangul?

Text, tables and pictures are kept, but complex layouts and some fonts can look slightly different. Check the PDF before submitting it.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The HWP reader runs inside your browser.
