One PDF from many photos

Forms and email often ask for “one PDF” instead of a pile of photos. This page puts every image you choose into a single PDF, one image per page, in the order you added them. Each page takes the size of its photo, so nothing is stretched or cropped.

How to make a PDF from images

  1. Choose your photos above, in the order you want the pages.
  2. PDF is selected and Combine into one PDF is on. Turn it off to get a separate PDF for each photo.
  3. Lower the quality to about 70% if the PDF must stay under a size limit.
  4. Press Convert and save the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use HEIC or PNG photos too?

Yes. Any image the converter can open can go into the PDF.

My photos are of paper documents. Can they look like a scan?

Use Photo to scan. It straightens the page and removes shadows before making the PDF.

Are transparent areas kept?

No. Transparent parts become white in the PDF.
