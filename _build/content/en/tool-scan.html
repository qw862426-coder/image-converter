When it helps

Many forms, contracts and expense reports still ask for a “scanned copy”. A normal phone photo of a page is usually tilted, has a shadow across it and shows the table underneath. This tool turns that photo into something that looks like it came out of a flatbed scanner, ready to email or upload as a PDF.

Choosing a look

OriginalKeeping the photo exactly as it was, only straightened and cropped.

Page size

Fit to page makes each PDF page the same shape as the paper in your photo, which suits receipts and odd-sized notes. A4 and Letter place every page on standard paper, which is better if someone will print it.

Frequently asked questions

What if the edges of the paper aren’t found?

Tap Adjust corners and drag the four dots onto the corners of the page. Auto detect tries again, and Whole photo uses the full picture without cropping.

How do I put pages in the right order?

Pages appear in the order you add them. Use the up and down arrows on each page to move it, and Rotate to turn it.

Can the text in the PDF be searched?

No. The tool cleans up the image but doesn’t recognize the letters, so the PDF contains pictures of the pages.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Edge detection, cleanup and the PDF are all done in your browser. To combine ordinary photos into a PDF without any cleanup, use JPG to PDF.
