One converter for everyday image formats

Phones, cameras, websites and design apps all save pictures in different formats, and the one you have is often not the one you need. This converter opens 17 image formats, including iPhone HEIC photos, Photoshop PSD files and PDF pages, and saves them as JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF and 12 other formats. You can convert one file or a whole folder’s worth in one go.

Popular conversions

  • HEIC to JPG: open iPhone photos on Windows, websites and older devices.
  • PNG to JPG: make large screenshots and graphics smaller.
  • JPG to PDF: send several photos as one document.
  • PDF to JPG: turn each PDF page into a picture you can post or paste.
  • Compress images: get under upload limits without changing the size in pixels.
  • PNG to ICO: make a favicon for your website.

What the quality setting means

JPG, WebP and PDF are “lossy” formats: they save space by leaving out detail that is hard to see. The Quality slider decides how much is left out. At 90% or more, most people can’t tell the result from the original. Around 70–80% the file is usually much smaller and still looks fine on a phone. Below about 50% you may start to see blocky patches. PNG, GIF, BMP and TIFF keep every pixel, so the slider is hidden for them.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on file size or number of files?

The site sets no limit. Because the work is done by your own device, very large images or hundreds of files at once can be slow on older phones. If that happens, convert them in smaller groups.

Why can’t I choose AVIF?

Saving AVIF depends on your browser. Opening AVIF works in most browsers, but only some can create it. If the option is greyed out, try the latest version of Chrome.

Do you keep a copy of my images?

No. The images are read and converted inside your browser and are never sent to a server, so there is nothing for us to keep.
