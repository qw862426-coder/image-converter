Which files can be converted

ProgramFile typesResult
Word.docxOne PDF page per document page
Excel.xlsx, .xlsm, .xls, .ods, .csvEach sheet as a table, wide sheets in landscape
PowerPoint.pptxOne landscape page per slide
Hangul.hwpPages in the paper size of the document

Older .doc and .ppt files and Hangul’s newer .hwpx format can’t be read yet. Open them in their program, save as .docx, .pptx or .hwp, and convert that file instead.

How the conversion works

When you press Convert to PDF, a document reader inside your browser opens the file, lays out each page the way the program would, and takes a sharp picture of it. The pictures are then put together into one PDF. Because of this, the PDF looks the same on every phone and computer, but the text in it is part of the picture and can’t be selected or searched.

Tips for a good result

  • Check the PDF before you send it, especially documents with text boxes, charts or unusual fonts.
  • If a font looks different, it isn’t installed on your device. Converting on a computer that has the font usually fixes it.
  • Very long documents can take a minute or two on a phone. Leave the page open until the progress bar finishes.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe for confidential documents?

The file is read only by your own browser and is never uploaded, so it doesn’t pass through anyone else’s server. As with any file, keep the PDF somewhere safe afterwards.

Can I convert several documents at once?

Yes. Add as many as you like; they are converted one after another, and each becomes its own PDF.

What about password-protected files?

They can’t be opened. Remove the password in the original program first.
