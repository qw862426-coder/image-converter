When removing the background helps

  • Profile pictures: replace a messy background with a clean colour or transparency.
  • Product photos: many online marketplaces require a white background.
  • Stickers, thumbnails and slides: a transparent PNG sits naturally on any background.

How to remove a background

  1. Open Remove background.
  2. Choose your photo.
  3. Pick High quality or Fast.
  4. Choose Transparent, White or Color for the background and save. Transparent is saved as PNG; white and colours as JPG.

The model is downloaded once and then kept by your browser, so later visits start much faster. Your photo isn’t uploaded; it is processed on your device.

Tips for cleaner results

  • Make the subject stand out from the background. A white shirt against a white wall is hard to separate.
  • Shoot in good light. Dark photos give blurry edges.
  • Simpler backgrounds work best. Leaves and busy streets are hard.
  • Fine hair, fur and glass are hard for any automatic tool. Try High quality.

A note on ID photos

Passport and ID photos have strict rules, and many authorities don’t accept photos with an edited background. Check the official rules first and use a photo studio if needed. For CVs and work profiles, which have no such rules, go ahead.
