When pictures work better than a PDF

  • Sending in a chat: messaging apps show pictures right away, while a PDF has to be opened.
  • Posting on social media or a blog: most only accept images.
  • Adding to slides: drop a table or chart from a PDF page straight into a presentation.
  • Upload forms that say “images only”.

How to convert

  1. Open PDF to JPG.
  2. Choose a PDF. You can add several at once.
  3. Pick a format. JPG is selected by default.
  4. Press Convert. Each page gets its own 1, 2, 3… save button.

Questions

How sharp are the images?

Pages are drawn at twice the normal screen size, which is clear enough for phones and slides.

Do Korean, Japanese and Chinese PDFs work?

Yes. The character data for these languages is included, so text shows correctly in most PDFs, even ones that don’t embed their fonts.

What about password-protected PDFs?

They can’t be opened yet. Remove the password first.

Going the other way

To turn several photos into one PDF, use JPG to PDF.
