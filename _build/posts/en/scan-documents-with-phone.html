Why people ask for a scan

Contracts, applications and expense receipts are still often requested “scanned”. A normal photo of a page is tilted, shadowed and shows the desk underneath, which makes it hard to read. With a few habits, a phone photo can look like it came out of a scanner.

How to take the photo

  • Use a dark surface: white paper on a darker surface makes the page edges much easier to find.
  • Shoot from straight above: a steep angle stretches the text.
  • Use good light and avoid shadows: keep the shadow of your phone and hand off the page; a window or a lamp helps.
  • Keep all four corners in the photo: a cut-off corner makes straightening harder.

How to make the scan

  1. Open Photo to scan.
  2. Choose your document photos, in page order if there are several.
  3. The page edges are found and straightened automatically. If they are wrong, tap Adjust corners and drag the four dots onto the corners of the paper.
  4. Choose a look and save as PDF.

Choosing a look

LookBest for
Black & whitePrinted text and signed forms. Smallest files, sharpest letters
GrayscalePencil writing and faint printing that might disappear in black and white
ColorReceipts, stamps, highlighter, photos and colored charts
OriginalKeeps the colours; only straightens and crops

Use Color for documents with a red stamp or seal. In black and white, red can fade or vanish.

Page size

Fit to page makes each PDF page the shape of the paper in your photo, which suits long receipts. Choose A4 or Letter if someone will print it.

Good to know

  • The text in the PDF is part of the image, so it can’t be searched or copied.
  • Your photos aren’t uploaded; everything happens on your device. That matters for IDs and contracts.
