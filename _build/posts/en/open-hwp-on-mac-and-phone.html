What is an HWP file?

HWP is the document format of Hangul (Hancom Office Hangul), a word processor made by the Korean company Hancom. It is used widely by Korean government offices, schools and companies, so official letters, application forms and school notices often arrive as .hwp files.

Macs, iPhones and Android phones don’t come with Hangul, and Microsoft Word and Google Docs can’t open HWP files properly.

Way 1. Convert it to PDF (nothing to install)

If you only need to read or print it, converting to PDF is easiest. PDFs open on every device.

  1. Open the HWP to PDF converter.
  2. Choose your .hwp file.
  3. Press Convert to PDF and save it.

The file is read only inside your browser and never uploaded. The newer .hwpx format isn’t supported yet, and complex tables or Hancom-only fonts may look slightly different.

Way 2. Install Hancom’s viewer app

Hancom offers a free viewer app for HWP files on the App Store and Google Play. If you receive HWP files often, it is handy to have. As the name says, it is for viewing; editing is limited.

Way 3. If you need to edit it

To fill in a form and send it back, you need Hangul itself, and Hancom Office for Mac is paid software. It is often easier to ask the sender for a Word (.docx) version: Hangul can save a document in Word format with “Save As”.

Which way should you choose?

Questions

What about .hwpx files?

Ask someone with Hangul to use “Save As → Hangul document (*.hwp)”. The new file can then be converted here.

Can I submit the PDF instead of the HWP?

Many offices accept PDF, but some require the original HWP file. Check the submission rules first.
