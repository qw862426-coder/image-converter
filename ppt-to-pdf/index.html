    Good to know

    • The PDF is built from page images, so text in it can’t be selected or searched.
    • Layout can differ a little from the original, especially for complex PowerPoint and Hangul files. Fonts that aren’t on your device are replaced.
    • Old formats (DOC, PPT) and HWPX aren’t supported. Save them as DOCX, PPTX or HWP first.

    Why share slides as PDF

    Presentation files are large and depend on the fonts and version of PowerPoint on each computer. A PDF opens on any phone or laptop and is the usual way to hand out slides after a class or meeting.

    How to convert PowerPoint to PDF

    1. Choose your .pptx files above.
    2. Press Convert to PDF.
    3. Save the PDF. Each slide becomes one landscape page.

    What to expect

    • Text, shapes, pictures, tables and slide backgrounds are included.
    • Animations, transitions, videos and speaker notes are not.
    • Complex charts, SmartArt and special effects may look different from PowerPoint.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I convert old .ppt files?

    Not directly. Open the file in PowerPoint, Keynote or another editor and save it as .pptx first.

    Can I edit the PDF afterwards?

    The pages are saved as images, so the text can’t be edited or selected.
