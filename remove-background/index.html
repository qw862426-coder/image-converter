    How it works

    The AI model looks at a smaller copy of your photo and estimates, pixel by pixel, how likely each part is to belong to the main subject. That estimate becomes a mask, which is cleaned up and enlarged to the size of your original photo. The mask is then used to cut out the subject from the full photo, so the result stays sharp: up to 4,000 pixels on the long side, which covers most phone photos.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my photo uploaded?

    No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser and runs there. Your photo never leaves your device.

    Can I choose a background color?

    Yes. Pick Color and choose any color, or White. These are saved as JPG; Transparent is saved as PNG.

    Why are some edges rough?

    Fine hair, fur, glass and busy backgrounds are hard for any automatic tool. Try High quality, or a photo where the subject stands in front of a plainer background.
